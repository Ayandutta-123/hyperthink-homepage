Flagship product

AI in a Box: your private AI stack, installed and running inside your own data centre

A pre-integrated stack of GPU infrastructure, open-weight models, retrieval, guardrails and ready-built applications — shipped, installed and supported by HyperThink. Your documents are indexed where they already sit. Nothing leaves your jurisdiction, and nothing is sent to a public model endpoint.

  • On-premise, air-gapped or private-cloud deployment
  • Open-weight LLMs and small language models, pre-loaded
  • Retrieval over your own documents and databases
  • Arabic and English out of the box
  • Role-based access, full audit logging, data-residency controls
  • Installed, tuned and supported by our Doha and Bangalore teams

Why HyperThink

The HyperThink difference

Your data stays in your jurisdiction

Every deployment is designed around the data-residency rules you actually have to satisfy, whether that is a Qatari regulator, a central bank mandate or an internal air-gap policy. Sovereign AI is the default here, not a premium add-on.

Agents that complete work, not chatbots that describe it

We build agentic AI applications that read a document, apply your policy and post the result into your system of record. The HR, invoice and accounts-payable agents in production today were built this way.

AI is only as good as the data layer underneath it

Our BI, data-warehousing and enterprise content management practices have run for years. We fix the data foundation first, which is why our AI projects leave the pilot stage.

Banking, government and energy, not generic enterprise

We work where approvals, audit trails and uptime obligations are real. Compliance, access control and logging are designed in at the start, not retrofitted before go-live.

A delivery team in your time zone

Engineering in Bangalore, delivery and account teams in West Bay, Doha. For one Saudi client we run a dedicated 8–10 person managed-services pod from India.
