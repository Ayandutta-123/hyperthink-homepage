client before launch; the prototype shows those items with a dashed red outline so they can't go live unnoticed.

**Primary keyword:** `sovereign ai` (India: 2,400 searches a month, difficulty 21).
**Secondary keywords:** `enterprise ai platform` (880), `ai consulting services` (1,000), `digital transformation company` (480).
**Long-tail keywords:** `sovereign ai india`, `generative ai consulting services`, `sovereign ai infrastructure`.
**Not targeted on the homepage:** `agentic ai`. Searches for it are mostly definitions and courses (difficulty 73), so it belongs in blog content.
